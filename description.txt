Bathyal

Bathyal is a massive world overhaul and expansion mod that transforms Terraria’s oceans from a shallow puddle at the edge of the map into a sprawling, terrifying, and beautiful subterranean frontier.

The surface world is only the beginning. Plunge into the lightless depths, master new underwater survival mechanics, and uncover the ancient secrets buried in the trench.
The Ocean Overhaul

The standard Ocean biome has been completely reimagined. The seafloor now drops away into expansive, deep-water cave systems that stretch toward the Underworld.

    The Twilight Zone: Descend 200 meters into a dimly lit aquatic cavern network teeming with bioluminescent flora and strange new aquatic life.

    The Midnight Trench: At 1,000 meters deep, the light completely fades. You will need specialized equipment just to see the horrors lurking in the pitch black.

    The Benthic Vents: Boiling hydrothermal vents where the ocean meets magma, harboring heat-resistant extremophiles and rare, volatile ores.

Deep Sea Mechanics

Standard water mechanics won't keep you alive down here.

    Crushing Pressure: Gills and basic breathing potions are not enough. Craft reinforced diving suits and specialized submersibles to withstand the immense pressure of the deep sea.

    Buoyancy & Currents: Master new movement accessories to navigate treacherous underwater slipstreams and avoid being dragged into the abyss.

    Light Deprivation: Flares and standard torches will fail. Utilize glowing algae, deep-sea crystal lanterns, and sonar-based weapons to map your surroundings.


    Developer Note: Bathyal heavily modifies world generation. We highly recommend creating a new world to experience all the new biomes and structures properly!
